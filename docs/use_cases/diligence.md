# M&A and investment diligence

Investment decisions are made on the basis of the information on hand as each decision is taken — yet the underlying data continues to evolve, making it impossible to reconstruct the decision context after the fact using conventional tools. Neotoma enables diligence teams to see exactly what had been recorded by the time each decision was taken, by versioning every finding, red flag, assessment, and data room artifact as immutable observations with deterministic provenance. When regulators, LPs, or internal committees ask why a deal was approved, the full information state can be reconstructed precisely as recorded by the IC vote.

## Entity examples

- `target_company`
- `finding`
- `red_flag`
- `assessment`

## Key question

> "What did we know about the target when the IC voted?"

## Data sources

- Data room documents (financial statements, cap tables, IP schedules)
- Third-party research reports and market analyses
- Management presentations and Q&A transcripts
- Background check and reference results
- Legal and regulatory filings
- Expert network call notes
